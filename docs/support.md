# Quietly support

Quietly: Friends & Focus is a free, open-source iPhone and iPad beta published by Botond Berde.

For help or privacy questions, email [botond.berde.dev@gmail.com](mailto:botond.berde.dev@gmail.com). For a public bug report, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/botondberdedev-cell/quietly/issues). Never include passwords, codes, private messages or private account screenshots in public reports.

Please include your iOS version, Quietly version/build from Settings, and the steps that caused the problem. Describe whether the problem involves Following, Stories, a profile video or a DM-shared video. Instagram can change its website at any time, so supported layouts may need filter updates.

Message alerts only detect supported unread-indicator changes while Quietly is open in an active visit. They do not provide background message delivery. Keep Instagram's own DM notifications enabled for messages while away. Recognized sponsored feed cards can be hidden; Story ads and unfamiliar layouts may still appear.

To sign out of the web session, use Settings → Clear Instagram login & website data. This does not delete your Instagram account.

Read the [privacy policy](privacy.md) and [source code](https://github.com/botondberdedev-cell/quietly). Quietly is independent of Instagram and Meta.
